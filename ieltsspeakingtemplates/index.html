Privacy Policy — IELTS Speaking Templates

Last updated: 2026-05-10

IELTS Speaking Templates (the "App") is published by huyvandev. This document explains what data the App collects, how it is used, and your choices.

1. Data we collect

The App does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information. There are no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and no crash-reporting services bundled in this version.

2. Local content

All cue cards, sample answers, IPA transcriptions and vocabulary are bundled with the App at install time and stay on your device. Nothing is downloaded from a server during normal use.

3. Local notifications

If you enable the optional "Daily reminder" feature, the App schedules a local notification on your device using the Android system scheduler. These notifications are generated entirely on your phone and never leave it. You can disable the reminder at any time in the App's Settings screen, or by revoking the notification permission in your Android settings.

4. Permissions

On Android 13 and later the App requests the POST_NOTIFICATIONS permission only when you enable the daily reminder. The App requests no other runtime permissions.

5. Children's privacy

The App is intended for general audiences (English learners) and does not knowingly collect any data from children. Because the App collects no data at all, there is nothing to delete or export upon request.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy is updated in the future, the new version will be published at the same URL with an updated "Last updated" date.

7. Contact

Questions or requests can be sent to huyvanorg@gmail.com.
