Privacy Policy — Sage Marketing

Last updated: 2026-06-28

Sage Marketing (also referred to as sage_mkt, the "Service") is operated by huyvandev. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use our multi-platform social media scheduling dashboard, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Who we are

Sage Marketing is a web application that lets you connect social media accounts, compose posts (including video and images), schedule publishing, and view basic analytics across supported platforms: Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn, and X (Twitter).

Contact: huyvanorg@gmail.com

2. Information we collect

We collect the following categories of data when you use the Service:

  • Account information — email address and authentication identifiers when you sign up or sign in to your Sage Marketing account (via Supabase Auth).
  • Connected social account data — when you authorize a platform via OAuth, we receive and store: platform user ID, display name, avatar URL, granted permission scopes, and encrypted OAuth access and refresh tokens. For TikTok, this includes basic profile information (user.info.basic) required to identify your account.
  • Content you create — post titles, captions, hashtags, per-platform variants, scheduled publish times, and media files (images and videos) you upload for publishing.
  • Publishing and analytics data — publish status, external post IDs, error messages, and engagement metrics (views, likes, comments, shares) retrieved from connected platforms after your content is published.
  • Technical data — server logs such as IP address, browser type, request timestamps, and error diagnostics needed to operate and secure the Service.

3. How we use your information

We use collected data solely to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Service;
  • Authenticate you and manage your account;
  • Connect and manage your authorized social media accounts;
  • Publish content to platforms you select, at the times you schedule;
  • Display publish status and basic analytics in your dashboard;
  • Refresh OAuth tokens so connected accounts remain active;
  • Respond to support requests and enforce our Terms of Service;
  • Detect, prevent, and address security issues or abuse.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data for third-party advertising.

4. Platform integrations and OAuth

When you connect a social account, you are redirected to that platform's official authorization page. We only receive the permissions you explicitly grant. You can disconnect any account at any time from the Service settings, which revokes our ability to publish on your behalf.

Platform-specific permissions we may request include:

  • TikTok — basic profile info and video publishing (user.info.basic, video.publish);
  • Meta (Facebook / Instagram) — page and content publishing permissions;
  • Google (YouTube) — video upload and read-only channel access;
  • LinkedIn — profile and member social posting permissions;
  • X (Twitter) — tweet and media upload permissions.

Each platform's own privacy policy governs data they collect during authorization. We encourage you to review those policies before connecting an account.

5. How we store and protect data

  • OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest using AES-256 before storage.
  • Media files are stored in secure cloud storage (Supabase Storage).
  • Database access is restricted to authenticated server-side processes.
  • Communication with the Service uses HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit.

While we implement industry-standard safeguards, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. You are responsible for keeping your account credentials confidential.

6. Third-party service providers

We use trusted infrastructure providers to operate the Service:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, and file storage;
  • Vercel — web application hosting;
  • Social platform APIs — Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn, and X — solely to perform actions you authorize (connect accounts, publish content, fetch analytics).

These providers process data on our behalf under their respective privacy policies and data processing terms. We do not share your content or tokens with any other third parties.

7. Data retention

  • Account and connected-account data are retained while your account is active.
  • Post content and media are retained until you delete them or close your account.
  • OAuth tokens are deleted when you disconnect a social account.
  • Server logs are retained for a limited period for security and debugging, then deleted or anonymized.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you;
  • Correct inaccurate information in your account profile;
  • Delete your account and associated data;
  • Disconnect any linked social media account;
  • Export your post content before deletion;
  • Withdraw consent for platform connections at any time via the platform's own settings or our dashboard.

To exercise these rights, contact us at huyvanorg@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

9. International data transfers

Your data may be processed on servers located outside your country of residence (including the United States and other regions where our cloud providers operate). We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place consistent with applicable data protection laws.

10. Children's privacy

The Service is not directed to individuals under 16 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The revised version will be posted at this URL with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
